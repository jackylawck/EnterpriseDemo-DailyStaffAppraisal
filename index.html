📌 專案摘要與架構定位 (Executive Summary & Sidecar Pattern) 📌 Executive Summary & Sidecar Architectural Positioning

This repository is an Enterprise Demo showcasing a Zero-License Edge Intake (Sidecar) Pattern using M365 and deterministic Governance-as-Code.

企業內部已建置運作穩定的既有核心績效評估系統（Core Appraisal System），但基於授權成本與資安隔離考量，並未開放前線日薪／工地員工帳號登入。EnterpriseDemo-DailyStaffAppraisal 在完全不更動既有核心系統、亦不增加前線員工軟體授權的前提下，透過 Microsoft 365 標準連接器（Forms、Power Automate、SharePoint Online 及 Outlook） 搭建自動化前置採集與加權定級層。

While the enterprise operates a mature Core Appraisal System for HR archiving and payroll reconciliation, frontline daily-paid site workers are not provisioned with M365 licenses or core system logins. EnterpriseDemo-DailyStaffAppraisal acts as a non-invasive Zero-License Edge Intake Layer—automating self-assessment capture, URI-encoded supervisor routing, deterministic zero-score enforcement, and weighted grading prior to verified core system entry.

在合規治理定性上，本系統完全基於確定性條件邏輯（Deterministic Conditional Logic）與獲授權監工的實質人工評核，不採用任何機器學習或生成式 AI 演算法，從架構源頭規避《歐盟人工智能法案》（EU AI Act）附件三高風險 AI 監管門檻，並全面對齊香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）、ISO/IEC 27001 及 ISO/IEC 27701 控制基準。

By relying strictly on deterministic conditional rules (Non-AI) and authenticated human-in-the-loop supervisory sign-off, the workflow intentionally remains outside the scope of High-Risk AI regulations (EU AI Act Annex III, ISO/IEC 42001) while enforcing strict compliance with Hong Kong PDPO (Cap. 486), ISO/IEC 27001, and ISO/IEC 27701.

🏛️ 流程生命週期與雙問卷接力閉環 (Workflow Lifecycle) 🏛️ End-to-End Workflow Lifecycle & Closed-Loop Relay

為解決前線員工「無企業帳號登入」與傳統紙本評核「難以落實權重與防呆」的雙重痛點，系統透過雙問卷與雙自動化流程實現端到端閉環管理：

To reconcile anonymous frontline accessibility with authenticated supervisory accountability, the system orchestrates two forms and two cloud flows into a closed-loop pipeline:

Step 01 • 問卷 A
手機掃碼免登入自評Anonymous QR Intake

公開問卷（Anyone can respond），4 節分支自動區分一般工種與工頭題目。Public form with 4-section conditional branching for general trades vs. foremen.

Step 02 • Flow 1
模板分流與預填網址Routing & Pre-Filled URL

正規化員工編號，自動指派 W1/W2 範本，組裝 URI 編碼連結電郵通知監工。Normalizes Staff ID, assigns W1/W2 template, and emails URI-encoded Form B link.

Step 03 • 問卷 B
監工登入驗證評核Authenticated Review

限內部帳號登入（Only people in my org），自動帶出工友資料並要求填寫創新實證。M365 login required; pre-fills worker context and captures Q03 innovation proof.

Step 04 • Flow 2
確定性防呆與自動定級Zero-Score Rule & Grading

第 3 題無具體事項自動覆寫為 0 分；SharePoint 即時計算加權總分與等級 A–E。Forces Q03 = 0 if unsubstantiated; calculates weighted total, %, and Grade A–E.

Step 05 • 核心系統閉環
HR 核對取數錄入歸檔HR Core System Entry

系統自動電郵通知 HR 及行政團隊核對計算結果，安全錄入既有核心評估系統。Notifies HR Admin with verified scores and direct list link for core system entry.

⚙️ 技術亮點與合規工程實踐 (Technical & Governance Highlights) ⚙️ Technical & Compliance Engineering Highlights

1. 零授權邊緣採集前置層Zero-License Edge Intake

在不更動既有核心人事系統、亦不購買前線 M365 授權的前提下，透過 Standard Connectors 實現前線掃碼採集與自動定級。Bridges non-licensed frontline workers with an existing core appraisal system using pure M365 Standard Connectors.

2. 實證強制零分防呆引擎Zero-Score Rule Engine

透過 contains(createArray(...), trim(toLower(coalesce(...)))) 過濾無效敷衍字眼，杜絕無實證「人情分」。Deterministically overrides Q03 rating to 0 when justification text is blank or matches placeholder blacklists.

3. 雙軌動態模板與分支轉型Dynamic W1/W2 & Null-Safe Cast

按職位自動路由 10 題（W1）或 15 題（W2）範本，並以 if(empty(...), 0, int(...)) 防止跳題空字串引發執行期報錯。Auto-routes W1 (100-pt) vs W2 (150-pt) templates and applies null-safe integer casting for skipped branch questions.

4. URI 編碼預填網址接力URI-Encoded URL Relay

利用 encodeUriComponent() 安全封裝中文姓名與職位參數，確保預填連結在桌面及手機版 Outlook 穩定開啟。Encodes Traditional Chinese parameters via encodeUriComponent() so pre-filled Form B links never break across mail clients.

5. 確定性非 AI 治理邊界Non-AI Regulatory Scope-Out

刻意採用透明確定性算術規則與人工主管簽核，主動排除歐盟 AI 法案（EU AI Act）高風險 AI 系統合規負擔。Transparent rule-based arithmetic with human-in-the-loop review intentionally avoids High-Risk AI regulatory overhead.

6. PDPO 與 ISO 雙標準對齊PDPO & ISO Alignment

前端嵌入雙語《收集個人資料聲明》（PICS），後端實施最小權限清單隔離與審計時間戳記，對齊 Cap. 486 與 ISO 27001/27701。Embeds bilingual PICS notices, enforces write-only ingress isolation, and aligns with HK PDPO Cap. 486 and ISO 27001/27701.
